How to play

Basics

  • Quick play pairs you with the next player waiting on the same mode and time control.
  • Create a room and share its 4-character code, or list it in the lobby. Open a full room to spectate.
  • Rated games need two signed-in players. Each mode has its own Elo and leaderboard, starting at 1200.
  • Computer plays classic chess at 8 levels and never changes your Elo.
  • Clocks start once everyone has moved, and until then a game can be aborted.

Modes

  • Classic standard chess.
  • Reconnaissance Blind you never see enemy pieces. Each turn, first sense a 3x3 window (or skip), then move or pass. A move that is impossible on the real board fails. Sliders stop at and capture the first hidden piece in their way. Both sides learn where captures happen, not what. No check: capture the king to win. Sensed pieces stay as faded ghosts.
  • Big Board 10x8 adds an archbishop and chancellor. 10x10 adds a second rank of cannon, camel, nightrider and grasshopper. Pawns promote to any piece their side started with.
  • 4-Player four armies on a cross-shaped board, turns go clockwise. A player who is mated, stalemated, flags or resigns is out and their army freezes in grey, free to capture. A king left in check can be taken before its owner moves. Pawns promote on the centre line. No en passant.
  • Free-for-all last king standing wins.
  • 2v2 partners sit opposite and can't capture each other. The first player out loses it for their team.

Twists

  • Holes walls in the middle. Nothing can enter or pass them.
  • Random armies back ranks rolled at random, about equal value, mirrored for both sides.
  • Swaps a piece type is replaced for both sides, e.g. knights become nightriders.
  • King of the hill get your king to one of the 4 centre squares to win.
  • Three-check give check 3 times to win.
  • Explosive captures a capture blows up both pieces and every non-pawn next to them. Blow up the king to win.
  • Drops pieces you capture join your hand. Drop one on an empty square instead of moving.
  • Bonus squares the first piece to land on a ★ uses it up. Knights and bishops become archbishops, rooks chancellors, queens amazons.

Twisted games are always casual.

New pieces

  • Archbishop bishop + knight
  • Chancellor rook + knight
  • Amazon queen + knight
  • Cannon moves like a rook, captures only by jumping exactly one piece
  • Grasshopper hops the first piece on a queen line, landing right behind it
  • Nightrider repeats a knight jump in a straight line until blocked
  • Camel leaps 3 squares one way and 1 the other
